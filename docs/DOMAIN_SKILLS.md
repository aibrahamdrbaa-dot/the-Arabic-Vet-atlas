# Domain-specific skills — final research supplement, 2026-11-26
These are candidates, not installed dependencies. Read actual SKILL.md, verify pinned revision and license, inspect scripts, then test on non-sensitive inputs. Prefer these over broad medical and game-art skills.
## Priority 1: anatomy and veterinary evidence
- K-Dense anatomist: https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agents/blob/main/scientific-agents/anatomist/skills/anatomist/SKILL.md — SKILL.md opened; MIT declared. Comparative morphology, NAV/UBERON, fixation shrinkage, segmentation artifacts, CT/MRI and specimen provenance. Excellent fit as a research/review guide; NOT qualified human approval.
- OpenVet Animal Meridian (formerly VetClaw): https://github.com/OpenVet-Projects/animal-meridian/blob/main/skills/literature/veterinary-pubmed-search/SKILL.md — SKILL.md opened; species filters and evidence discovery. Related reviewed pages: https://github.com/OpenVet-Projects/animal-meridian/blob/main/skills/literature/evidence-grading/SKILL.md and https://github.com/OpenVet-Projects/animal-meridian/blob/main/skills/literature/comparative-medicine-search/SKILL.md . Use search discipline, not clinical grading rules as a substitute for anatomy evidence. Do not send nonpublic records to VetEvidence or other external services.
## Priority 2: image segmentation and geometry
- pieper/slicer-skill: https://github.com/pieper/slicer-skill/blob/main/SKILL.md — opened; source and extension retrieval, MRML, VTK/ITK, segmentation, Slicer Python. Requires setup/retrieval, not a standalone segmentation engine. Inspect license and setup.sh first; use authorized public datasets only.
- EL4CTEO blender-core: https://github.com/EL4CTEO/blender-skills/blob/main/skills/blender-core/SKILL.md — opened, MIT declared, Blender 5.2+ required. Headless scripts and API patterns; verify environment.
- EL4CTEO blender-asset-qa: https://github.com/EL4CTEO/blender-skills/blob/main/skills/blender-asset-qa/SKILL.md — opened, MIT declared. Mesh metrics and multi-angle review. Adapt: do NOT automatically fill anatomical foramina or cavities, join separate structures, move anatomy to remove apparent floating parts, or use gaming tolerances as scientific criteria.
- kajisho5/blender-skill: https://github.com/kajisho5/blender-skill/blob/main/SKILL.md — opened, Blender 4.2+; headless inspect/convert/verify/optimization; inspect license and scripts before adopting. Useful alternative if pinned version compatible.
## Selection decisions
Use the anatomist + veterinary PubMed search as references for the project's original veterinary-terminology, evidence-traceability and scientific-qa skills. Use Slicer and pydicom only when raw image data is available; begin with existing segmented specimen files, not automated segmentation of a complete animal. Use headless Blender QA and Khronos glTF validation for asset pipeline. No procedural generation, automatic topology repair or decimation without a specimen-specific evidence check. See SKILLS_RESEARCH.md for general scientific and web-engineering skills. All sources linked rather than copied, and no scripts installed/executed.
