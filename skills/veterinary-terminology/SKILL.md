name: veterinary-terminology
description: Create or review Arabic, English and Latin veterinary anatomical concept records.
---
Use NAV edition and section as nomenclature authority; use canine-specific anatomy evidence for descriptions and laterality. Distinguish pelvic/thoracic limb, cranial/caudal, dorsal/ventral, palmar/plantar. Use stable concept IDs, not labels; record Arabic synonyms and Latin spelling. Map Uberon only when concept and species match. Each factual assertion needs a page/DOI or source locator; mark unknown. Compare independently with a veterinary reviewer before approval. Never translate human anatomy as canine anatomy.
