name: anatomy-audit
description: Verify specimen-derived anatomy and rights before adding educational assets.
---
Inspect original dataset, paper, file list, license, author, specimen, side, units and orientation. Download in cloud only when permitted; calculate SHA256, inventory meshes, inspect renders and distinguish paths/segmentation/approximations. Record missing structures; never infer completeness. Require expert review before publishing; preserve attribution and transformations.
