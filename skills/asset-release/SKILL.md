Read data contract and science policy. Separate source and derivative hashes, original license and attribution, redistribution and modification rights. Record specimen metadata, transforms, units, version and stable node bindings. NC/SA/ND conditions assessed individually. Drafts remain outside public. Require independent human scientific and rights review with evidence; CI is not a legal or scientific certification. Verify Khronos validation, no external URIs, no orphan files, and derivative checksum before release.
