---
name: headless-geometry
description: Process authorized CT-derived or scanned veterinary geometry reproducibly in a cloud sandbox.
---
Preserve raw assets and source hashes outside Git. Inspect actual units, coordinate frame, laterality, mesh count and topology; never guess scale. Use isolated pinned Blender headless with --python-exit-code 1 and explicit arguments, disable auto-run. Export GLB with persistent node IDs, source-to-output transforms, logs and checksums. Compare before/after dimensions, landmarks and six views; do not decimate until anatomical features pass review. Use Khronos validator and glTF Transform selectively; test decoder support before compressed delivery. Do not merge unrelated specimens.
