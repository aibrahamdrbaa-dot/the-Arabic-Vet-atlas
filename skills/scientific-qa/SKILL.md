---
name: scientific-qa
description: Review veterinary anatomy records and 3D specimens for evidence, consistency and scientific errors.
---
Check species, sample, age, breed, side, orientation, origin/insertion, relationships, labels, missing or duplicate anatomy and changes from source. Compare claim-by-claim to canine references, not human atlases. Audit actual geometry from multiple views; list uncertainty and conflicts. Human veterinary expert approval is separate from agent critique. Never self-mark expert-reviewed. Record correction and affected IDs.
