---
name: evidence-traceability
description: Find veterinary anatomical publications and produce traceable claim evidence without inventing citations.
---
Define species, region, claim and evidence needed. Search PubMed, Crossref, Figshare, Zenodo, university repositories and citation chains. Record query/date, DOI, file list, source license, specimen and exact supporting section. Distinguish abstract from full text, source geometry from interpretation. Verify DOI and claim independently. Reject inaccessible evidence as definitive. Output claim-to-source table and gaps; never copy restricted text.
